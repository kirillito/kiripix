# M6 gate: a district without the Owner, style that holds, NPCs from a base, and the game's M06–M10 needs

Spec §14 (M6). The Owner's sign-off; no agent does this. Claude does every CLI step first and leaves the Owner the eye checks
and the choices.

## What to do

- **Sources for the district:** `to-paint` for the district's rooms ([0349](0349-m6-to-paint-pack.md)), the Owner paints
  from its folder, and `inbox` ([0350](0350-m6-source-inbox.md)) files every image. Recorded: the Owner's minutes per Source,
  and how many images `inbox` couldn't match.
- **A district without the Owner:** the ch01 rooms that have painted Sources (at least five, the Owner's choice which)
  through [0331](0331-m6-first-passes-over-a-list-of-rooms.md)'s batch, from one command. Recorded: each room's stop,
  tokens and Decisions.
- **One sitting:** the Owner clears the district's queue in the UI ([0329](0329-m6-ui-review-queue.md)). Recorded: the
  sitting's minutes from `stats --match`, and per room, against M5's 2.95 minutes for one room and the laneway's 19.23.
- **The district view** ([0330](0330-m6-ui-district-view.md)) on the district: every Exit paired or marked `one_way`, then
  `export --match` and the walk test across two joined rooms in Godot.
- **Style:** the Owner's references picked; `check --all` on the cast and the rooms, with every `style.*` warning recorded
  here as a true or a false alarm. A known mismatch (a sprite imported at 2x, from 0332) is caught. If the false alarms are
  few, the Owner says which codes become errors (0324's Q5).
- **NPCs:** the work 0336 filed, to the Owner's answers: at least a dozen background NPCs from one base, with no new render,
  in a ch01 room in Godot, drawn by the game's `CharacterSprite` with their colours. Recorded: their atlas cost (none new) and
  the Owner's minutes for the dozen.
- **Room normals** ([0341](0341-m6-room-normals-stage.md), [0342](0342-m6-export-room-normals.md)): a ch01 dungeon room with
  `mode=depth` in Godot, lit by a moving `PointLight2D` (the game's flashlight if its M10 has one), and the Owner's eye on it.
- **Named Masks** ([0345](0345-m6-export-named-masks.md)): a room with `cover` painted on whole cells; the game's grid reads
  `masks/cover.png` (or a headless Godot test does, if the game's M09 isn't there yet).
- **Effects:** what [0347](0347-m6-vfx-trial.md) chose, built: the game's M06 hit spark and the Jazz Musician's sound wave in a
  battle in the laneway, in Godot.
- The agent acceptance test, its tokens against M5's.
- The full suite's time, after a batch, against M5's.
