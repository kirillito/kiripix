# M2 gate: Layers, Props and the Manifest, in the real game

Spec §14 (M2). The owner's sign-off; no agent does this.

## What to do

- Rebuild `room.harbor_front` from `layers` down, then lift at least one real object in it with the real Runners, in the UI.
- Take one Prop through chroma key and one through matting, and instance both in a test scene.
- Open the room in Godot 4.7 and walk behind and in front of the Layer.
- Place a light and an emitter in that room, and look at them in Godot.
- Delete a test Asset and prune it. Export one Asset on two game branches, and merge them.
- Prepare a new reference room to a finish you would ship, by hand, and note your own total time. Use
  `references/subway-lower-bay.jpeg` from the game repo unless you pick another.
- Check the results of [0057](0057-m2-agent-acceptance-test.md), [0062](0062-m2-claude-first-pass-trial.md) and
  [0063](0063-m2-multi-screen-source-trial.md).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An M1 room with no layer Decisions rebuilt with nothing to review, and re-exporting it wrote nothing.
- [ ] The lifted object: the Plate behind it is acceptable, the actor sorts correctly against its line in Godot and in the
      walk test, and the two agree.
- [ ] An "always above" Layer stays above the actor.
- [ ] Both Props show at the right place, with collision at the footprint and the origin where it was set.
- [ ] A light shows at its position, colour and radius. An emitter node is there with its metadata, and loads without an error.
- [ ] `export --prune` removed the deleted Asset's files, refused an edited one, and Godot was happy with any leftover
      `.import` sidecar.
- [ ] A real two-branch merge of the game: the driver resolved the Manifest, and the next export was clean.
- [ ] The agent acceptance test passed, and its token count is recorded against M1's 7,629 fresh tokens.
- [ ] Time per room is recorded: the reference room's journal span and the Owner's total time, against the M1 baseline in
      [0037](0037-m2-layers-and-props.md). No threshold yet: M2 sets the second data point.
- [ ] [0062](0062-m2-claude-first-pass-trial.md)'s result is recorded, with the Owner's call on what it means for M4.
- [ ] [0063](0063-m2-multi-screen-source-trial.md)'s result is recorded, and spec §16's open decision 7 says which method
      continuous rooms use, or that the game uses Exits only.

## Outcome

Record here, not as criteria: the `solid` collision polygon count on the room (1,533 convex pieces at the M1 gate; tolerance
floods may raise it), and the SAM 2 timings from [0059](0059-m2-slow-click-to-segment.md) and
[0053](0053-m2-ui-layers-screen.md).
