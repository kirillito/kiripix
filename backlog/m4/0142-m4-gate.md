# M4 gate: a large room ready for a grid battle

Spec §14 (M4). The Owner's sign-off; no agent does this.

## What to do

- Make a ch01 room of about 2 x 2 screens with `extend`, from `canvas` to `done`, and take it to `export` with a battle area
  (`battle.*`, cell 32) and one Look with its own `solid`.
- In Godot 4.7, check that the game can find the area, read `masks/solid.png`, and show the Look and enable its collision.
- Record your own time on the room, split into extending and the rest, against M2's and M3's time per room. If
  [0182](0182-m4-measured-owner-time.md) has landed, record `kiripix stats <id> --json` here as well (minutes per Stage,
  Decisions, undos) and compare it with your own figure. Making the pieces happens outside Kiripix, so extending stays
  by hand.
- Run the agent acceptance test ([0141](0141-m4-agent-acceptance-test.md)) and record its tokens.
- If [0134](0134-m4-trial-room-extended-both-ways.md) filed the SAM 2 crop ticket, check clicks on the gate's room with it.
- Rebuild `character.mc02` (or its revision) with `palette` `parts` set, export it, and in Godot swap one part's row of
  `ramps.png` with a test shader ([0147](0147-m4-character-palette-ramps.md)).
- The characters after the M3 gate ([0161](0161-m4-characters-after-the-m3-gate.md)): cut mc02's idle to what the game plays
  by changing its range on the clips screen, and check the transitions and the flatter look the trials (0158, 0160) chose, in
  Godot beside a painted room.
- Record `kiripix --help`'s and the full suite's times after [0163](0163-m4-start-faster.md).
- A sprite ([0174](0174-m4-sprite-kind.md)): a raccoon (or Sanjay) from a PixelLab export, in 4 directions, imported, fitted,
  exported, and placed in Godot beside `character.mc02` under a `PointLight2D`, loaded by the same code as the character.
  Record the Owner's time for it.
- The game's four M03 bodies (2 genders x 2 outfits, [0166](0166-m4-ramps-match-across-characters.md)): fill the ramp table,
  rebuild and export, and in Godot give all four the same skin and hair replacement with one shader. If the trial
  [0170](0170-m4-weapon-in-a-battle-clip-trial.md) chose (a), one attack per class with its weapon; if 0172 is in M4, a walk's
  `foot` frames against the frames by eye.
- Rooms that move ([0183](0183-m4-ambient-animation.md)): in the kitchen, the coffee's steam from an emitter with Kiripix's
  shape, direction and colours, plus one colour cycle and one frame loop, exported and played by the game from its frame counter.
  Record the Owner's time for each.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The room is in Godot, with the results above recorded here.
- [ ] Swapping each part's ramp in Godot recolours that part and no other pixel, in every direction of every clip.
- [ ] On every character in the Project with `parts`, a part is the same row of `ramps.png` with the same length, and
      `check --all` raises no `palette.parts_differ`.
- [ ] A clip's range changed after export keeps its animation names; `sheet.part_vanishes` is quiet on the revised Model, or
      its findings are accepted in writing.
- [ ] The Owner's choices from the trials 0158 (transitions) and 0160 (a flatter look) are built, or moved to a later milestone
      in writing.
- [ ] The sprite's `character.json` loads with the game's character loader unchanged, its outline matches mc02's colour and
      width, and it is lit by the room's lights.
- [ ] Each Loop's frame 0 is pixel-identical to the room, a capture at the same tick is byte-identical across two runs, and
      `loop.flash` is quiet.
- [ ] Every finding the gate raises is filed as a ticket or accepted in writing.
