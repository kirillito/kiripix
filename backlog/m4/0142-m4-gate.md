# M4 gate: a large room ready for a grid battle

Spec §14 (M4). The Owner's sign-off; no agent does this.

## What to do

- Make a ch01 room of about 2 x 2 screens with `extend`, from `canvas` to `done`, and take it to `export` with a battle area
  (`battle.*`, cell 32) and one Look with its own `solid`.
- In Godot 4.7, check that the game can find the area, read `masks/solid.png`, and show the Look and enable its collision.
- Record your own time on the room, split into extending and the rest, against M2's and M3's time per room.
- Run the agent acceptance test ([0141](0141-m4-agent-acceptance-test.md)) and record its tokens.
- If [0134](0134-m4-trial-room-extended-both-ways.md) filed the SAM 2 crop ticket, check clicks on the gate's room with it.
- Rebuild `character.mc02` (or its revision) with `palette` `parts` set, export it, and in Godot swap one part's row of
  `ramps.png` with a test shader ([0147](0147-m4-character-palette-ramps.md)).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The room is in Godot, with the results above recorded here.
- [ ] Swapping each part's ramp in Godot recolours that part and no other pixel, in every direction of every clip.
- [ ] Every finding the gate raises is filed as a ticket or accepted in writing.
