# M3 gate: one character in Godot, and a UI that keeps up

Spec §14 (M3). The Owner's sign-off; no agent does this.

## What to do

- Take one real character (Q4 of [0097](0097-m3-characters.md): a Quaternius base body and outfit, joined into one `.blend`) from `import` to `export` in the UI: the Mixamo round trip if
  it needs one, clips `idle`, `walk` and `attack`, the `top34_8` preset, with real Blender 5.2.
- Open it in Godot 4.7 in a test scene: play each clip in each of the 8 directions, walk it across a `ch01_dungeon` room
  with a `PointLight2D`, and watch it in front of and behind a lifted Layer.
- Note your own total time on the character, and how long the Mixamo round trip and the renders took.
- Use the first-wave fixes on a `ch01_dungeon` room you haven't prepared, and open the room list in the art Project.
- Check the results of [0106](0106-m3-outline-method-trial.md) and [0116](0116-m3-agent-acceptance-test.md).

## Acceptance criteria

**The character**
- [ ] One character plays idle, walk and attack in 8 directions in Godot 4.7, each direction facing the right way, at the
      clip's speed and looping as set.
- [ ] The feet stay at the scene's origin: no swimming in idle, and `sheet.pivot_drift` passes on every stationary clip.
- [ ] Every frame is on the palette, with binary alpha, and the outline is steady from frame to frame.
- [ ] The normal map lights the character from the light's side, in every direction.
- [ ] It sorts against a room's foreground Layer as the Body does.
- [ ] A re-export writes nothing; renaming an exported clip is refused.

**The first wave**
- [ ] The room list and a room open in a few seconds in the art Project, with something on screen at once
      ([0090](0090-m3-status-without-re-deriving-lifts.md), [0083](0083-m3-ui-show-that-work-is-happening.md)).
- [ ] Lifting a Layer, accepting and running `palette` each show they are working; a Layer can be removed; the findings
      list reads as something to act on ([0083](0083-m3-ui-show-that-work-is-happening.md),
      [0091](0091-m3-remove-a-layer.md), [0092](0092-m3-ui-layer-screen-legible.md),
      [0093](0093-m3-ui-findings-a-person-can-read.md)).
- [ ] Rooms after `palette` read as pixel art, and r02's seam is gone ([0094](0094-m3-palette-stage-muddies-rooms.md)).
- [ ] A multi-screen room can be moved around when zoomed in ([0082](0082-m3-ui-canvas-navigation.md)).
- [ ] An accepted matting result stays accepted ([0095](0095-m3-matting-result-changes-after-accept.md)), and a two-branch merge
      of an exported Asset needs no hand-picked image ([0096](0096-m3-merge-exported-images.md)).

**Measures and trials**
- [ ] The agent acceptance test passed, and its token count is recorded against M2's 16,941 fresh tokens.
- [ ] Time per character is recorded: the journal span and your own total, with Mixamo and render time apart. No threshold
      yet: this is the first data point.
- [ ] Time per room on the fresh `ch01_dungeon` room is recorded, against M2's 75 minutes for r03 by hand.
- [ ] [0106](0106-m3-outline-method-trial.md)'s choice is in spec §16 decision 3.

## Outcome

Record here, not as criteria: render time per frame on the GPU and CPU, peak VRAM during a render, the character's LFS size
(see [0098](0098-m3-blender-renders-and-pinning.md)), and how long the room list took to open before and after 0090.
