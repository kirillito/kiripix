base and rigged by Mixamo (Q4 of [0097](0097-m3-characters.md)), or the Owner's revision of it. The gate briefly used the
test fixture instead (2026-11-10), until mc02 existed.

## What to do

- Take the real character from `import` to `export` in the UI: the Mixamo round trip, clips `idle`, `walk` and `attack`, the
  `top34_8` preset, with real Blender 5.2.
- Open it in Godot 4.7 in a test scene: play each clip in each of the 8 directions, walk it across a `ch01_dungeon` room
  with a `PointLight2D`, and watch it in front of and behind a lifted Layer.
- Note your own total time on the character, and how long the Mixamo round trip and the renders took.
- Use the first-wave fixes on a `ch01_dungeon` room you haven't prepared, and open the room list in the art Project.
- Check the results of [0105](0105-m3-toon-shading.md), [0106](0106-m3-outline-method-trial.md) and
  [0116](0116-m3-agent-acceptance-test.md).

## Ready for the gate (prepared 2026-11-10)

Every ticket in `depends_on` is done except [0105](0105-m3-toon-shading.md)'s band sheets and the trial
[0106](0106-m3-outline-method-trial.md), both back from M5 on 2026-11-11, and [0116](0116-m3-agent-acceptance-test.md): its harness and dry run pass, and the
real run is yours, because it spends money: `uv run python -m tests.acceptance.run --milestone m3`. After the run, compare
its fresh tokens with M2's 16,941. A real agent may try `next`'s `decide … clips` template with a made-up `source` before asking you. A
failed attempt there adds tokens, so look for it in the record before putting the rise down to the character.

For the Godot session:
- **What export writes:** under `export.godot.root`, `character/<name>/` holds `<name>.tscn`, `frames.tres` (a
  `SpriteFrames`), `texture.tres` (a `CanvasTexture`), `color.png` and `normal.png`. The Manifest is
  `manifest/character.<name>.json`, at schema 2, with `ids.clips`.
- **The scene:** a `Node2D` root at the feet, with an `AnimatedSprite2D` child named `Sprite`. Animations are named
  `<clip>_<dir>`, so a test script plays them with `$Sprite.play("walk_sw")`. The directions are
  `s sw w nw n ne e se`.
- **The normal map:** 0113 checked headlessly, with Godot 4.7.2 at `C:\Homespace\[software]\Godot\`, that it survives
  `AtlasTexture` over `CanvasTexture` under a `PointLight2D`. The check passed in both Compatibility and Forward+. The lit
  look in each direction is still yours to judge.
- **The outline:** 1 px, outside the silhouette, in `finish`'s `outline_colour`. That is an index into the Project palette,
  and it defaults to 0. `placement=edge` is the alternative. A pose that reaches the cell's edge has no line on that side.
- **Pivot drift:** `sheet.pivot_drift` has a tolerance of 0. If real Blender renders jitter by 1 px on idle, it fails, and
  the fix is a `pivot_tolerance` param on `sheet`. mc02's first run (2026-11-11) did: 179 `sheet.pivot_drift` errors of 1 px
  on Mixamo's idle, as the hips sway, and 14 `sheet.frame_overflow` on the attack, which reaches outside the 78 x 78 cell.
- **In the UI:** the frame strip, compass, player and onion skin are on `render`, `palette`, `finish` and `sheet`. The keys
  are: number pad for direction; `,` and `.` to step a frame; Up and Down to change clip; Space or `P` to play; `O` for onion
  skin; `N` for normals; `Z` for zoom. The normal view shows the normal frames only. It has no light that follows the mouse.
