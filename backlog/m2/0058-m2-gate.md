# M2 gate: Layers, Props and the Manifest, in the real game

Spec §14 (M2). The owner's sign-off; no agent does this.

## What to do

- Lift at least one real object in a `ch01_dungeon` room with the real Runners, in the UI. Use one object [0076](0076-m2-claude-layers-first-pass-trial.md)
  didn't lift. `room.harbor_front` was the room here until the Owner made it throw-away, 2026-11-06.
- Take one Prop through chroma key and one through matting, and instance both in a test scene.
- Open the room in Godot 4.7 and walk behind and in front of the Layer.
- Place a light and an emitter in that room, and look at them in Godot.
- Delete a test Asset and prune it. Export one Asset on two game branches, and merge them.
- Prepare a new reference room to a finish you would ship, by hand, and note your own total time. Use
  `references/subway-lower-bay.jpeg` from the game repo unless you pick another.
- Check the results of [0057](0057-m2-agent-acceptance-test.md), [0062](0062-m2-claude-first-pass-trial.md) and
  [0063](0063-m2-multi-screen-source-trial.md).

floods may raise it), and the SAM 2 timings from [0059](0059-m2-slow-click-to-segment.md) and
[0053](0053-m2-ui-layers-screen.md).

- **The acceptance test:** passed, 16,941 fresh tokens against M1's 7,629, about 2.2 times, with the lift as the extra work
  ([the record](../../docs/acceptance/2026-11-05-m2.md)).
- **`solid` collision pieces,** from each room's `room.json` before the gate's lift: r01 35 (and 6 `pit`), r02 61, r03 155. M1's
  `harbor_front` had 1,533.
- **SAM 2, hover to preview on the GPU** (0053): cold server 1.84 s, warm 0.045 to 0.05 s, straight after a rebuild 1.39 s, after a
  trim 1.20 s. 0059's `masks` numbers are beside them in 0053's table.

**The Owner's gate (2026-11-07).** The Owner closes the gate, with the UI's usability as M3's concern: "issues with UI usability are
piling up, and we need to address them. Otherwise, it's growing more and more unusable and slow."

- **Time per room:** `room.ch01_dungeon_r03` by hand, Masks and Layers, 75 minutes, the Owner's figure. M1's baseline was 13.4 minutes

    [0092](../0092-m3-ui-layer-screen-legible.md), with 0083 and [0089](../0089-m3-time-a-lift-honestly.md).

**Session checks (2026-11-07).** Evidence for the Owner's sign-off, not the sign-off. Run in a throwaway clone of the game at
`7025c04` and a copy of `room.ch01_dungeon_r02` and the lamp, so nothing here is in the real repos. Godot 4.7.2.

  front of `bench`, and the top of the actor cut by `machine` with "always above" on it, from south of its line. Nobody has walked it,
  and the UI's walk test hasn't been compared.

  lamp composited on `#00FF00` with a hard edge, so it's an easy case for the key, not a generated Source.
